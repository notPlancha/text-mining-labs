## Recommended Textbooks

There are 3 recommended textbooks; **you only need one**:

1. **The Oxford Handbook of Computational Linguistics** (2nd edn)
   - Author: Ruslan Mitkov
   - Publisher: Oxford University Press, 2014
   - ISBN: `9780199573691`
2. **Practical Text Analytics: Interpreting Text and Unstructured Data for Business Intelligence (Marketing Science)**
   - Author: Steven Struhl
   - Publisher: Kogan Page, 1st edition, 2015
   - ISBN: `978-07499474010`
3. **Deep Text: Using Text Analytics to Conquer Information Overload, Get Real Value from Social Media, and Add Bigger Text to Big Data**
   - Author: Tom Reamy
   - Publisher: Information Today Inc, 2016
   - ISBN: `9781573875295`

## Use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) Tools

The course encourages using AI tools to improve learning outcomes (e.g., questioning your understanding, exploring alternative solution approaches, and clarifying concepts) and sharing experiences in the Canvas discussion forum.

### Mandatory Requirements & Academic Integrity

- **Full Understanding**: You must be able to **explain your solutions and answers in detail**. Submitting a solution that you cannot explain may be reported as potential plagiarism/cheating.
- **Explicit Acknowledgment**: Be transparent and acknowledge any AI tool usage in your submission.
  - *Example acknowledgment*: `"In this exercise, we used Google for retrieving relevant references about activation functions; Microsoft Word for auto-correcting the spelling, ChatGPT for checking the text for unclear statements."`
- **Verify Outputs**: GenAI tools frequently make mistakes even when outputs look convincing. The course references [this ZDNet/ACM article](https://www.zdnet.com/article/chatgpt-answers-more-than-half-of-software-engineering-questions-incorrectly) noting that ChatGPT (3.5) answered more than half of software engineering questions incorrectly.
