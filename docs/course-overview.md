# Course Overview — Text Mining (D7058E)

- **Course**: Text Mining `D7058E 27014 HT2026` (Canvas ID: `29006`)
- **Canvas Modules (ILOs & Modular Videos)**: <https://canvas.ltu.se/courses/29006/modules>

This course covers modern Natural Language Processing (NLP), including large language models (LLMs) such as ChatGPT and Gemini, alongside foundational text mining concepts. Some modules follow a flipped-classroom format requiring students to watch modular videos before class for in-class discussion.

## Course Rules & Policies

- **Lab & Quiz Prerequisite**: All labs (or projects) and quizzes together make up **3.5 credits** and are a **prerequisite for the oral exam**.
- **Group vs. Individual Work**: All labs/projects are **group tasks (2–3 people)** except for the **last lab (Lab 4)**, which is **individual** and required for all students.
- **Lab Presentations & Submissions**:
  - During lab sessions, all groups present their **Colab notebook (not slides)** in **4 minutes**, and all group members are expected to be present.
  - Each group also submits their Colab notebook on Canvas.
- **Late Submission Policy**: Late lab submissions receive grade **G with an observation** (*"Next year, capped at G even if worth a VG"*). See [Lab Grading Criteria](lab-grading-criteria.md) for presentation and grading details.
- **Projects**: Students choosing a project are responsible for securing an evaluation date with the teacher before the oral exam.
- **Grading Scale**: Quizzes, labs, projects, and the oral exam use the 3-grade scale **U, G, VG** (from 2027).

## Oral Exam

- **Dates**: **13, 14, and 15 January 2027** (sign-up page and practice questions will be shared later on Canvas).
- **Format**: Approximately **5 questions** randomly selected from a pool of about **50 questions**.
- **Re-exam**: May be re-conducted only when convenient for both the examiner and students in the next study period (LP).
