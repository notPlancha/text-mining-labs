# Welcome

> Source: <https://canvas.ltu.se/courses/29006/pages/welcome>

Warm welcome. This course gets you up to speed with the latest trends in Natural Language Processing, including large language models (LLMs) like ChatGPT or Gemini without neglecting the basics of understanding these modern trends.

Important to note:

- Intended learning outcomes (ILOs) and **modular videos** (complementing the classes) can be found in the [modules](https://canvas.ltu.se/courses/29006/modules).
- The schedule for all sessions is [here](https://canvas.ltu.se/courses/29006/assignments/syllabus). All classes will be in **A3002 (except Nov 3 in A3101c @ 10.15AM)** and labs in **A3012.**
- Zoom link for all sessions: <https://ltu-se.zoom.us/j/67325566799>
- Kindly note:
  - Some modules require students to watch the modular videos before class (as flipped classroom) to have discussions in class.
  - All labs (or projects) and quizzes make up 3.5 credits and are pre-requisite for the oral exam.
  - Late lab submissions are given the grade **G with an observation (Next year, capped at G even if worth a VG)**. Grading criteria are [here](https://canvas.ltu.se/courses/29006/pages/lab-grading-criteria).
  - All labs/projects are group (2-3 people) tasks except for the last one, which is individual and required for all.
  - Lab classes are for all groups to present their colab (not slides) in 4 minutes and all are expected to be present. Each group also submits their colab.
  - It is the responsibility of students doing projects to secure a date for their project evaluation by the teacher before the oral exam.
  - A sign-up page will be shared later for the oral exam and practice questions made available.
  - Oral exam will be 13, 14 & 15th Jan, 2027. About 5 questions are randomly selected from a pool of about 50 questions.
  - Oral exam may be re-conducted only when convenient for both examiner & students in the next LP.
  - We recognise some work full time but **endeavor to put in the required time to study**, watch the videos and do assignments to pass the course.

You will find additional course information [here](https://canvas.ltu.se/courses/29006/pages/course-information), project suggestions [here](https://canvas.ltu.se/courses/29006/pages/group-project-suggestions), and recordings of class and lab sessions will be [here](https://canvas.ltu.se/courses/29006/pages/recordings-of-class-and-labs) later.
