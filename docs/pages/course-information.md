**Use of Artificial Intelligence Tools**  
Artificial intelligence (AI) tools are increasingly useful. As students, engineers and scientists we should learn to use them efficiently. Thus, we encourage the use of AI tools for the purpose of improving your learning outcome in this course and we ask you to share your experiences in this discussion forum so that we can learn from each other. Please note that you must be able to explain in detail your solutions and answers to exercises and problems in the course. If you submit a solution that you cannot explain we may need to report it as a case of potential plagiarism/cheating. Instead, use Generative AI (GenAI) tools to question your understanding, get ideas for different solution approaches, or get further information about concepts of interest. Be transparent and acknowledge the use of AI tools. For example, the acknowledgment of a lab submission could be something like "In this exercise, we used Google for retrieving relevant references about activation functions; Microsoft Word for auto-correcting the spelling, ChatGPT for checking the text for unclear statements". Be aware that GenAI tools frequently make mistakes although the outputs may appear convincing. For example, in the ACM article described [here](https://www.zdnet.com/article/chatgpt-answers-more-than-half-of-software-engineering-questions-incorrectly) it was found that ChatGPT (3.5) answers more than half of software engineering questions incorrectly.
