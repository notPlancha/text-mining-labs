# Lab Grading Criteria

> Source: <https://canvas.ltu.se/courses/29006/pages/lab-grading-criteria>

**Grading questions (for both lab tasks & projects):**

1. Show your code outcome, go through it and explain what it does in about 5 mins.
2. Is there any part of the code that may be optimized (made better)?
3. What other challenges can this solution be adapted for?
4. Will you like to discuss the time & space complexities of parts of your solution (optional)?

**Additional requirements for any group projects:**

- Show appropriate text pre-processing
- Perform adequate analysis (e.g. visualization) of your data before training the model
- Use suitable metric(s) to show the performance of your system
- Discuss the disadvantages of the metric you use
- Understand & discuss some challenges (e.g. bias) of the model.
- About 20mins is allocated for the presentation of your project (in January, 2026 - tentative date)
