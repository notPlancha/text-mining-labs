# Lab Grading Criteria

Source: Canvas pages [`lab-grading-criteria`](https://canvas.ltu.se/courses/29006/pages/lab-grading-criteria) and [`welcome`](https://canvas.ltu.se/courses/29006/pages/welcome).

## Presentation Format

- Each group presents their **Colab notebook directly (no slides)** in **4 minutes** (noted as ~5 minutes in the grading criteria page) during the lab session in **A3012** / Zoom.
- All group members are expected to be present.
- Each group also submits their Colab notebook on Canvas.
- Each group must document **which part each member completed** inside the notebook, along with an **AI usage declaration** if AI tools were used.

## Grading Questions (Labs & Projects)

During the lab presentation, be prepared to discuss the following 4 core questions (plus any additional relevant follow-up questions):

1. **Show your code outcome, go through it and explain what it does** in about 5 minutes.
2. **Is there any part of the code that may be optimized (made better)?**
3. **What other challenges can this solution be adapted for?**
4. **Will you like to discuss the time & space complexities of parts of your solution (optional)?**

## Additional Requirements for Group Projects

If doing a group project instead of standard labs, your work must additionally:

- Show appropriate text pre-processing.
- Perform adequate analysis (e.g., visualization) of your data before training the model.
- Use suitable metric(s) to show the performance of your system.
- Discuss the disadvantages of the metric(s) you use.
- Understand & discuss challenges (e.g., bias) of the model.
- Prepare for an allocated **~20-minute presentation** (listed in Canvas as January 2026 tentative date — verify current 2027 schedule with the teacher).
